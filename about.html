About

Hugo V. Monteiro is a Portuguese heavy-metal guitarist with a career shaped by studio recordings, live shows and the European metal circuit.

He spent around eleven years with Attick Demons, contributing guitar to the albums Atlantis and Let’s Raise Hell. The band’s work connected him with notable musicians from the international metal scene, including Paul Di’Anno and Ross the Boss on Atlantis, and Chris Caffery on the title track of Let’s Raise Hell.

His live history with Attick Demons includes a special guest show with Paul Di’Anno in Corroios in February 2009, a direct support slot for W.A.S.P. in Lisbon in November 2012, and European festival appearances at Swordbrothers Festival in Germany and MetalDays in Slovenia.

Mindfeeder

Since 2018, Hugo has been guitarist with Mindfeeder, continuing his work in heavy metal.

Other projects

His wider musical history also includes Live After Demons, an Iron Maiden tribute project, and Theriomorphic.

This site brings together selected career milestones, recording credits and live history. Dates and credits are presented where they can be substantiated.
